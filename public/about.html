About S.G. Associates

Civil construction expertise serving residential, commercial, and institutional clients across NCR.

Company Profile

S.G. Associates is a Gurugram-based proprietorship firm specializing in all types of civil works. Operating from Ganga Vihar, Phase-3, Gurugram, the firm coordinates skilled and unskilled teams, supervisors, material suppliers, and architects to deliver reliable construction outcomes.

This web portal transforms manual word-of-mouth operations into a structured digital presence, enabling clients to browse services, inspect past work, and submit tracked enquiries around the clock.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a dependable name in civil construction by delivering work that is durable, safe, economical, and completed on time.

Mission: To execute every assignment according to accepted engineering practice, maintain transparent client dealings, utilize resources efficiently, and adopt modern digital tools.

Core Values

Quality

Adherence to structural specifications and sound construction practice in every assignment.

Integrity

Honest communication regarding project cost, execution schedule, and scope of work.

Timeliness

Structured planning and coordination so milestones committed to clients are met.

Safety

Protection of site workers, visiting clients, and the surrounding community.

Continuous Improvement

Adopting modern engineering methods and information technology to elevate service quality.
